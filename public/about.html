About Horror Haven

Horror Haven exists because horror fandom deserves a real hangout spot, not a subreddit thread that dies after a day or a Discord server you need an invite link for. It's a live chat community built specifically for people who are into horror movies and games — genuinely into them, not just during October.

What's here

  • A General room for whatever's on your mind
  • Dedicated rooms for specific movies and games — anyone can start one
  • A Support room for when something you watched or played actually got to you
  • A VIP Lounge for Premium members

How it's funded

Horror Haven runs on three things: Premium memberships ($4.99/mo, ad-free plus VIP access), affiliate links on the Movies and Games pages (a small commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you), and the occasional ad for free-tier users. No data is sold to anyone.

Community expectations

Be a decent person. A profanity filter runs automatically, but it's not a substitute for basic respect — harassment, hate speech, and targeted cruelty get you reported and banned, filter or no filter. The Support room in particular is meant to be a safe place to land; treat it that way.

Who's behind it

Horror Haven is built and run by Sam Developments. If something's broken or you have an idea, the Support room is the fastest way to reach the team.

Horror Haven — a community, not a corporation. Built by fans, for fans.
