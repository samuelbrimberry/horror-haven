News & Updates

What's changed on Horror Haven, straight from the person building it.

September 16, 2026

Moderation tools and reporting are live

Every message now has a report button. Reports land on the admin dashboard along with one-click actions to delete the message, ban the user, or mark it resolved. A profanity filter also runs automatically on every message — no one gets banned just for cursing, it just gets starred out.

September 16, 2026

Anyone can start a room for their favorite movie or game

Chat rooms used to be fixed. Now you can spin up a dedicated room for any movie or game on the spot — Hereditary and Scream rooms already exist because members made them. A new Settings page also lets Premium members manage or cancel their subscription directly.

September 16, 2026

Premium memberships are real

Go Premium now runs through actual Stripe Checkout — $4.99/month unlocks the ad-free experience and the VIP Lounge. Cancel anytime from Settings.

September 16, 2026

Horror Haven is live

After months of horror fans getting scattered across dead subreddit threads and Discord servers nobody could find, Horror Haven opened its doors: live chat rooms for movies, games, general horror talk, and a support room for when something actually got under your skin. Free to join.

Horror Haven — a community, not a corporation. Built by fans, for fans.
